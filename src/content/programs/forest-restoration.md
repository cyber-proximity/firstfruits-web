Our initiatives extend to large-scale forest restoration, using biochar to enrich soil and accelerate ecological recovery. Each tree planted is a step towards a healthier planet, creating vibrant, biodiverse ecosystems for future generations.

## Our work

- Community tree-planting days
- Native species nurseries
- Protecting and restoring natural habitats
- Linking restoration with livelihoods such as agroforestry
