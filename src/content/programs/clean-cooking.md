Millions of families still cook over open fires or inefficient stoves. The smoke harms health, especially for women and children, and the demand for firewood drives deforestation.

Clean cooking solutions save families money on fuel, improve health, reduce emissions and create new economic opportunities. Time once spent collecting fuel can go towards education, livelihoods and rest.

## Our work

- Distributing efficient cookstoves with local partners
- Training local entrepreneurs to build and maintain stoves
- Education on the health benefits of clean cooking
