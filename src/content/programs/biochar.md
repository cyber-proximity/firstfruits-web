Biochar is a stable form of carbon produced from biomass. Added to soil, it acts as a carbon sink, locking away atmospheric carbon dioxide while dramatically improving soil fertility and water retention.

This simple yet profound technology offers a tangible way to reverse environmental degradation, improve agricultural resilience and contribute to a regenerative future. It embodies the principle of working *with* nature, transforming waste into a valuable resource for planetary healing.

## What we do

- Train communities in small-scale biochar production
- Support farmers in applying biochar to restore depleted soils
- Combine biochar with forest restoration and tree planting
