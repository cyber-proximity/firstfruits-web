Discover the profound impact of plant-rich eating on your health and the planet. A plant-based diet reduces your carbon footprint, supports biodiversity and extends compassion to all living beings.

## How we promote plant-based living

- Community cooking workshops and shared meals
- Simple, nourishing recipes using local produce
- Celebrations such as World Vegan Day
- Resources on nutrition and mindful eating

Learn more on our [Plant-Based Living](/plant-based-living) page.
