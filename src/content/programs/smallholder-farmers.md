Climate change presents unprecedented challenges, and smallholder farmers who rely on nature's rhythms feel them first. Unpredictable weather, prolonged droughts and new pest pressures threaten food security and push communities towards unsustainable practices.

First Fruits believes in a sacred stewardship of the Earth. We work alongside farmers to understand the specific vulnerabilities of each region, and to co-create adaptation strategies that farmers lead themselves.

## How we help

- Training in regenerative and climate-smart farming techniques
- Revitalising traditional knowledge that is adapted to local ecosystems
- Peer-to-peer learning circles and shared resources
- Access to biochar and soil-restoration methods
